GrowcerySG

A marketplace to get more people in Singapore growing and buying food locally, with a carbon tracker that shows what buying local saves.

Context
Business Application Development, Nanyang Polytechnic (team project)
Year
2025 to 2026
Stack
C#, ASP.NET Web Forms, SQL Server, JavaScript

My part

  • Marketplace and trading. The produce marketplace, plus the peer-to-peer side where growers trade with each other directly.
  • Engagement. Quizzes, achievements, leaderboards and rewards, to give people a reason to come back.

The carbon tracker

Most of Singapore's food is imported, so buying local has a measurable effect on emissions. The tracker works out how much CO2 a purchase saves, using published transport emission factors and real import distances into Singapore, rather than a flat estimate.
