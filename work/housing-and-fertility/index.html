Housing and Fertility

Singapore's birth rate keeps falling while housing keeps getting more expensive. This study asks how closely the two track each other.

Context
Business Analytics Project, Nanyang Polytechnic
Year
2025
Tool
Tableau

The data

  • HDB resale prices and the HDB resale price index.
  • Median income by age group.
  • The national total fertility rate.

The method

Housing costs today affect decisions about children a few years later, so I ran a lagged comparison rather than lining the years up directly. The analysis focuses on the 25 to 29 and 25 to 34 age groups, the years when most people are buying a first flat and starting a family.

I then split the housing side by flat type, floor area, storey range and lease remaining, to see which parts of the market moved most.

The output

An interactive Tableau dashboard story that traces how housing has changed across the years and across age groups, set against the fertility rate.
