SCI Agent Copilot

A copilot for insurance agents that searches the policy base, checks messages against MAS disclosure rules, and flags where two versions of a document disagree.

Context
Business Innovation Project, Nanyang Polytechnic
Year
2026
Stack
React, Vite, Supabase, Capacitor

The problem

Insurance agents were digging through policy documents by hand to answer client questions. That is slow, and the cost of a mistake is real: a client message that misses a compliance rule can cost the firm money.

What I built

  • Knowledge retrieval. Search across the policy base, so an agent finds the right clause without opening every document.
  • Compliance checks. Messages are checked against MAS disclosure rules before they reach a client.
  • Document drift detection. When two versions of a document disagree, the copilot flags where.
  • Shipped as an installable PWA with a service worker, so agents can add it to their home screen like a native app.

A hole I closed

While building sign-up I found that anyone could register as a policy handler with full write access. I moved role creation to a locked-down server-side flow, so a role can only be granted, never claimed at sign-up.

I also made the audit trail append-only. Records cannot be edited after the fact, which is the point of an audit trail in a regulated business.
